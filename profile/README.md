# Tidelight

### Intelligence for markets that never sleep.

Tidelight is an agentic market intelligence platform that connects live context, source-led research, exposure mapping, scenario analysis, and paper-first decision workflows for US equities and tokenized instruments.

## The Tidelight method

**Event → Evidence → Exposure → Scenario → Decision → Execution**

One connected ecosystem for asking better questions, understanding what moves markets, and staying in control of the next decision.

## Platform

- **Research** — Turn a plain-language question into a source-led market brief.
- **Exposure** — Map market narratives to issuers, sectors, catalysts, risks, and tokenized instruments.
- **Agent Flow** — Follow how research agents gather context and build a considered view.
- **Trading workspace** — Explore paper-first strategies and live-ready decision workflows.
- **Decision history** — Keep the reasoning, evidence, and changing context visible over time.

## Developer ecosystem

Tidelight is building a professional developer layer for market intelligence: documented APIs, secure API keys, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) access for products and agents that need reliable US equity and tokenized-market context.

Visit the [Developer platform](https://tidelight.app/developers) to explore the ecosystem and integration path.
